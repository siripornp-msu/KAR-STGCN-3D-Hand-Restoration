% --- [PATCH 1: Section 3.3 (End of Section) — Strict End-to-End Causality] ---
To guarantee strict end-to-end causality in System B, first-order velocities are initialized causally at window boundaries ($\Delta \hat{\mathbf{X}}_1 = \mathbf{0}$), batch normalization layers freeze population running statistics during streaming inference, and coordinate denormalization uses the causal scale $d_{\text{ref}}^{\text{causal}}(t)$ and anchor $\mathbf{p}_{t,0}^{\text{valid}}$. Automated causal invariance testing (\texttt{causality\_test.py}) verifies bit-exact output identity ($\|\hat{\mathbf{X}}_{1:t}(\mathbf{X}_{1:T}) - \hat{\mathbf{X}}_{1:t}(\mathbf{X}'_{1:T})\|_{\infty} = 0$) under arbitrary perturbations of future frames $t' > t$.

% --- [PATCH 2: Section 4.1 — Subject-Independent Cross-Validation Accounting] ---
All evaluations strictly enforce \textbf{5-Fold Subject-Independent Cross-Validation} across four benchmarks categorized by sensing modality, ensuring zero subject overlap between training, validation, and testing partitions ($\mathcal{S}_{\text{train}} \cap \mathcal{S}_{\text{val}} \cap \mathcal{S}_{\text{test}} = \emptyset$; verified in \texttt{subject\_split\_manifest.csv}):
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{SHREC'17 [6] (Depth-Based Skeletal Benchmark; 30 fps):} All 2,800 raw sequences (100\% retention) across $N=28$ independent subjects ($df=27$; 14-class and 28-class protocols; 3 folds of 6 test subjects and 2 folds of 5 test subjects, averaging $2,240.0$ train/val and $560.0$ test sequences per fold).
    \item \textbf{FPHA [7] (Cross-Modal 6-DoF Magnetic Benchmark with Synthetic Missingness; 30 fps):} All 1,175 raw sequences (100\% retention) across $N=6$ subjects and 45 action classes ($df=5$; grouped subject-independent partitioning with 4 folds holding out 1 test subject and 1 fold holding out 2 test subjects, averaging $940.0$ train/val and $235.0$ test sequences per fold).
    \item \textbf{LMDHG [5] (First-Generation Optical LMC Benchmark; 60 fps):} 608 segmented dynamic gesture instances extracted from 50 continuous recordings across $N=21$ subjects ($df=20$) and 13 classes (4 folds of 4 test subjects and 1 fold of 5 test subjects, averaging $486.4$ train/val and $121.6$ test instances per fold).
    \item \textbf{Orthogonal Dual-LMC2 (ML2HP) [18] (Cross-View Optical LMC2 Hardware Benchmark; 120 Hz):} From 714,000 raw multi-view frame observations (357,000 synced stereo pairs), deterministic extraction of 2 non-overlapping 60-frame windows per subject-posture trial yields $21 \times 17 \times 2 = 714$ evaluation windows. Excluding 9 windows (1.26\%) with simultaneous dual-sensor dropouts yields 705 valid cross-view windows evaluated across 5 subject-independent folds (averaging $564.0$ train/val and $141.0$ test windows per fold).
\end{enumerate}

% --- [PATCH 3: Section 4.4 — 7-Stage Scientific Evidence Validation Pipeline] ---
All baselines use identical corrupted inputs, visibility masks $\mathbf{V}$, subject splits, and tuned hyperparameters ($\text{lr} = 1\times 10^{-3}$ for SAITS [16]; $2.5\times 10^{-3}$ for GCNs over 40 epochs, batch size 16, across 5 seeds: 42, 100, 2026, 777, 999). To ensure full auditability, our public repository (Release Tag \texttt{v2.0.0}) implements a \textbf{7-Stage Scientific Evidence Validation Pipeline}: (1) \textit{Data Provenance \& Split Audit} (\texttt{dataset\_manifest.csv}, \texttt{subject\_split\_manifest.csv}); (2) \textit{Reference Reliability Audit} (\texttt{reference\_quality\_report.csv}) quantifying Dual-LMC2 Kabsch residuals across central ($1.62 \pm 0.35$ mm) vs.\ boundary ($2.38 \pm 0.49$ mm) workspace zones; (3) \textit{Causality \& Leakage Audit} (\texttt{causality\_test.py}); (4) \textit{Automated Table Synchronization} (\texttt{seed\_results.csv}, \texttt{verify\_tables.py}); (5) \textit{Statistical Validation} (\texttt{statistical\_validation.ipynb}); (6) \textit{Failure Boundary Matrix} (\texttt{robustness\_matrix.csv}); and (7) \textit{30-Minute Continuous 120 Hz Stress-Test Logs} (\texttt{deployment\_benchmark.csv}, confirming 0\% frame drops and stable 245 MB VRAM utilization over 216,000 continuous frames).
